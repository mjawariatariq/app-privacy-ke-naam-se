# Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to delete your data associated with our application, you can follow these steps:

1. Go to your Facebook Account's **Settings & Privacy** > **Apps and Websites**.
2. Locate our application in the list of active apps.
3. Click the **Remove** button to disconnect the app and delete your data from our system.

Alternatively, you can contact us directly at your-email@example.com to request manual data deletion.
